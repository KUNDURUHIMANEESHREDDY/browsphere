# Catalog isolated recovery v13.6

The v13.5 live trace showed the generic objective recovery replacing an exact chip-filter proposal with a return to the retailer homepage. The research specialist also denied every SetChecked action. v13.6 isolates the catalog contract from generic replacement and stale-control replans; a narrow Research exception requires a fresh snapshot, successful live binding, the native catalog filter route, and the exact catalog gate. Existing live policy and authorization checks still apply. No consent, account, payment or unrelated checkbox is authorized. Filter telemetry now reflects observed checked-state readback, not a proposed action.

The catalog supports the original exact query, shorter model query, broad product-family query, a bounded non-submitting search-button fallback tied to a native entry receipt, and at most two observed same-retailer category destinations with relevant family/model tokens. The alternative routes cannot license an unmatched product or prove that a product exists. Every product still requires retained exact source evidence; each retailer is revalidated independently, and final comparison requires both. Missing evidence remains unverified, not a claim that all stock is unavailable.
