# Dynamic catalog discovery (v13.4)

The v13.3 public Level 4 diagnostic observed Croma exposing a visible div combobox labelled search, rather than an input. Reliance Digital search results exposed a large filter panel before any captured product link. The fix prioritizes bounded rendered same-origin product-path links, opens exact observed custom search controls, excludes filter search inputs, allows a bounded hydration observation window after acknowledged Enter, and recognizes primary rendered product title elements on product paths. Product heading and selling-price evidence remains scoped, exact-source and subject to matching specifications; a crossed-out price cannot serve as the selling price. No guessed product URL, hidden API, credential, pincode or transaction action is used. The catalog task may use up to 44 steps including observation waits; other tasks retain the original budget.

Regression: a browser fixture with 160 checkbox filters retains the product link in the 120-control observation budget; a separate fixture distinguishes a marked selling price from a crossed-out original price. The file fixture validates the visible link label, not file:// navigation, which is forbidden. The actual two-retailer benchmark predicates remain unchanged. Public diagnostic probes are collected only for isolated Level 4 tests. A concurrent visual-coordinate executor change was already present in the working tree; its disabled-control regression now checks the guard before both normal and visual click dispatches. Runtime executor behavior was not changed by that regression correction.

Limits: product discovery still depends on actually rendered public links and the compiled two-retailer contract. A candidate link is not proof of matching specifications or current price, and unavailable/ambiguous evidence must not produce a verified comparison. No full-suite live benchmark or release installer verification is implied by unit/fixture success.
